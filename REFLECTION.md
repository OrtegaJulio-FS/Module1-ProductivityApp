# Assignment 1.6 — Personal reflection (complete after testing)

This is a writing guide, not a finished reflection. Replace these prompts with 300–500 words in your own voice. Describe only experiences you actually had, and follow your course's AI-assistance disclosure policy.

1. Cross-platform challenges (about 100–125 words): Explain differences you observed between phone and browser, especially SQLite/SecureStore versus web storage, interactions, layouts, and version compatibility. Give a specific problem and how you verified the fix.
2. Time constraint (about 75–100 words): Describe your actual work order, time spent, scope decisions and why you chose dark mode. Avoid claiming three days of work if that did not occur.
3. Improvements (about 75–100 words): Discuss realistic next steps such as editing tasks, due dates, automated integration checks, or accessibility refinements, and explain their value.
4. Surprises (about 75–100 words): Describe what you personally found easier or harder than expected, with an example from testing.
